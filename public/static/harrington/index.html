Before You Call.

If your question is not answered here, the office will answer it directly.

Call the office directly or submit the consultation form on our contact page. We respond to new enquiries within one business day and can usually offer an initial meeting within the same week, in person or by video.

Bring any contracts, correspondence, filings or notices relevant to your matter, along with a short timeline of events if one is easy to assemble. If documents are not to hand, come anyway — we can begin with the facts as you understand them.
